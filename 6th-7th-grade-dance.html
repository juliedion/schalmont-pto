Schalmont Middle School · 6th & 7th Grade

🌟 6th & 7th Grade Dance

Friday, March 12, 2027 · 7:00–9:00 PM · Schalmont Middle School · with DJ Ketchup

🌟

Celebrating our 6th & 7th graders!

📅
DateFriday, March 12, 2027
🕖
Time7:00 – 9:00 PM
📍
LocationSchalmont Middle School
🎧
MusicDJ Ketchup
🗳️ 6th & 7th graders, you pick the theme!

Theme to be chosen by the 6th & 7th grade classes

We'll be polling the 6th and 7th grade classes so they can choose this year's theme. The winning theme will be announced here once the votes are in.

Get ready for a night of music and dancing with your friends, with DJ Ketchup spinning the tunes! The PTO will share more information, volunteer opportunities and details as the dance gets closer.

🙋 Parents & families

Interested in volunteering?

It takes a team to make the dance a success: helping with check-in, snacks, decorations, setup, cleanup and chaperoning. Sign up below to let us know you're interested in helping, and we'll reach out with details as the dance gets closer.

Volunteer sign-ups are opening soon! In the meantime, email julie@schalmontpto.com to let us know you'd like to help.
