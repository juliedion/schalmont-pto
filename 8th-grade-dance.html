Schalmont Middle School · 8th Grade

🌟 8th Grade Dance

Friday, June 11 or 12, 2027 (date TBD) · 7:00–9:00 PM · Schalmont Middle School

🌟

Celebrating our 8th graders!

📅
Date · TBDJune 11 or 12, 2027
🕖
Time7:00 – 9:00 PM
📍
LocationSchalmont Middle School
🙌
Co-chairsAmber Szady & Sharon Moriarity
🗳️ 8th graders, you pick the theme!

Theme to be chosen by the 8th grade class

We'll be polling the entire 8th grade class so they can choose this year's theme. The winning theme will be announced here once the votes are in.

We're thrilled that the district has placed the dance under the direction of the Schalmont PTO this year. The PTO, along with co-chairs Amber Szady and Sharon Moriarity, will share more information, volunteer opportunities and fundraising details at the Fall PTO Meeting.

🙋 Parents & families

Interested in volunteering?

It takes a team to make the 8th Grade Dance a night to remember: helping with check-in, food, decorations, setup, cleanup, chaperoning and fundraising. Sign up below to let us know you're interested in helping, and we'll reach out with details.

Volunteer sign-ups are opening soon! In the meantime, email julie@schalmontpto.com to let us know you'd like to help.
