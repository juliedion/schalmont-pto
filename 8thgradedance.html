Celebrating our 8th graders!

DateSaturday, June 12, 2027
Time6:00 – 9:00 PM
LocationSchalmont Middle School Gymnasium2 Sabre Lane, Schenectady, NY 12306 · Map
Co-chairsAmber Szady & Sharon Moriarty
This year's theme

A Night Under the Stars

The votes are in! We asked the 8th grade class to help plan their dance, and they chose A Night Under the Stars over Hollywood / Red Carpet, Black & Gold and Masquerade.

Join us as we celebrate the Class of 2031 on Saturday, June 12, 2027, from 6:00 to 9:00 PM in the Schalmont Middle School Gymnasium (2 Sabre Lane, Schenectady, NY 12306).

The dance is run by the Dance Team Leadership this year, led by co-chairs Amber Szady and Sharon Moriarty. Thank you to every 8th grader who shared their ideas!

Share your Sabre’s Story

Help us celebrate the Class of 2031! Upload photos of your 8th grader, from baby pictures to today, add a special message from family and/or friends, and share a few fun facts about them if you'd like. The photos will be shown in a slideshow during the dance and at graduation. Each student has their own folder. Parents and guardians: find your 8th grader’s folder below (not there yet? create one for them), confirm you’re their parent or guardian, and you can share as many times as you like.
