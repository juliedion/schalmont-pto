Help & How-To

Everything here is written for volunteers, not web designers. If you get stuck, the AI Assistant can walk you through any of this.

The big picture

The back office is a private area. The public can't see anything you do here until you publish it. Your work is organized by school. Pick your school from the left menu, then use the tabs: Pages, Files & Photos, and Planning.

Building a page

  1. Open your school → Pages tab → ＋ New page.
  2. Type a Page title (e.g. "Jefferson Fun Run 2026"). The Page link fills in automatically — you can shorten it.
  3. Add blocks one at a time using the buttons:
    • Heading — a title for a section.
    • Paragraph — normal text. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
    • Bullet list — type one item per line.
    • Photo — pick from photos you've uploaded, or paste an image link.
    • Button — a big clickable button (e.g. "Sign up now") that links somewhere.
    • Divider line — a thin line between sections.
    • Embed — drops in a Google Form, Google Calendar, or a YouTube video. Paste the share link.
  4. Watch the Live preview on the right — that's exactly what visitors see.
  5. Press Save draft as you go. Nothing is public yet.
  6. When it's ready, press Publish. You'll get a link like schalmontpto.com/p/jefferson-fun-run to share by email or Facebook.
  7. To take it down later, open it and press Unpublish. To fix a typo, just edit and press Update live page.

Reordering or removing blocks

Each block has small ▲ ▼ arrows to move it up or down, and an ✕ to delete it.

Uploading files & photos

  1. Open your school → Files & Photos tab.
  2. Click the upload box (or drag files onto it). Photos, PDFs and Word docs up to 15 MB.
  3. Each file gets a Copy link button. Paste that link into a page, an email, or a Facebook post.
  4. Photos you upload here automatically appear as choices when you add a Photo block to a page.
  5. Delete removes the file everywhere — any page using it will show a broken image, so check first.

Forms and sign-up sheets

A built-in builder is coming in the next update. For now, the quickest way:

  1. Go to forms.google.com and build your form or sign-up (it's free).
  2. Click Send → the link icon → Copy.
  3. In your page, add an Embed block and paste the link. The form now appears right inside your PTO page.

The Planning checklist

Each school has a shared to-do list on the Planning tab. Everyone who helps with that school sees the same list. Add tasks, check them off, remove them when done. Great for "who's bringing what".

The AI Assistant

Ask it how to do anything here, or ask it to write things for you — reminder emails, newsletter blurbs, social posts, thank-you notes, event ideas. It gives you words and steps; you still do the clicking. It never sees private student or family data.

Adding administrators (owner only)

  1. The new person first creates a normal account at the sign-in page.
  2. An owner opens People & Roles, finds them, sets their role to School administrator, and checks the school(s) they should manage.
  3. Press Save. They now see those schools when they open the back office.

Common questions

I published a page — where is it?

At schalmontpto.com/p/ followed by the Page link you chose. It's also listed (with a View button) on your school's Pages tab.

Can I put my page in the top menu of the website?

Not yet from here — ask the site owner to add it to the navigation. You can link to it from any existing page or from Facebook right away.

I made a mistake on a live page.

Open it, fix it, press Update live page. Changes are instant.

Did I break the website?

No. The back office can only add new /p/ pages and files. It can't touch the existing site pages.
