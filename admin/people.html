People & Roles

Officers and administrators: their organization, title, and what they can do in the back office.

How roles work:
• School administrator — can build pages, upload files, and plan for the school(s) you check below. Nothing else.
• Owner (super-admin) — full access to every school and this People & Roles page.
• Member — a directory account with no back-office access.

This list shows officers and administrators only. Turn on Show all directory accounts if you need to promote someone who signed up on their own.

Create a login for someone

Makes their account and password so they can sign in right away. If they already have an account this just updates their role/title. Tell them to change the password after their first sign-in ("Forgot password" on the sign-in page).
