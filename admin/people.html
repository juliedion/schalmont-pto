People & Roles

Decide who can use the back office and which school(s) each person manages. Only the PTO president / owner sees this page.

How roles work:
• School administrator — can build pages, upload files, and plan for the school(s) you check below. Nothing else.
• Owner (super-admin) — full access to every school and this People & Roles page. Give this to co-presidents only.
• Member — a regular directory account with no back-office access.

People who signed up themselves appear in the list below. To create a login for someone (so they don't have to sign up), use the box just below.
