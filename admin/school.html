Everything for this school in one place. Use the tabs below for pages, files, ideas and the to-do list — and the “sections” group in the left sidebar for Events, Clubs, Fundraising, and everything else.

A page is a web page on the PTO site — like a flyer that lives online. You build it by stacking simple blocks (a heading, a paragraph, a photo, a button…). Nobody can see it until you press Publish. After that it has its own link you can share, e.g. schalmontpto.com/….

Sorting never hides anything — every event, club, program and page for this school is always listed below, current and past. Type/Status only narrow the list if you change them.

This is your school's link library — a saved list of photos and documents you can reuse on pages, in emails, and in Facebook posts. You add things by pasting a link to them.

Where do I get a link?
• A photo already on the web (our site, a news article, a vendor's site): right-click it → Copy Image Address.
• A PDF or flyer (permission slip, menu, order form): put it in Google Drive, click Share → Anyone with the link → Copy link.
• A photo from your computer: upload it to a free host like postimages.org and copy the "Direct link", or ask the site owner to add it to schalmontpto.com/images.

Tick "This is a photo" for images so they appear in the page builder's Photo block.
