A page is a web page on the PTO site — like a flyer that lives online. You build it by stacking simple blocks (a heading, a paragraph, a photo, a button…). Nobody can see it until you press Publish. After that it has its own link you can share, e.g. schalmontpto.com/….

Drag a card to change where it appears on the homepage. Cards in the gray box below never show on the public page — use Move to Present to bring one back.
