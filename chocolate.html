Schalmont PTO Fundraiser

Gertrude Hawk Chocolate Bar Fundraiser

For Schalmont Middle and High School families — sign up to sell chocolate, or find out where you can buy a bar in person. Every purchase supports PTO programs.

Start Selling See Where We're Selling
In partnership with Gertrude Hawk Chocolates

How Student Selling Works

We're keeping this simple and easy to track for everyone.

  • 1Students and families can request a starter quantity of chocolate to sell.
  • 2Rather than handing out a full 48-bar carrier, the PTO distributes manageable batches — typically 12–24 bars to start.
  • 3Sellers return payment according to PTO instructions.
  • 4Once your first batch is sold and accounted for, additional chocolate can be requested.
  • 5Inventory is limited and distributed while supplies last — this system helps us keep accurate records and reduce lost or unpaid product.
