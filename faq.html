Jefferson Elementary PTO

Woestina Pre-K & Jefferson Elementary

When and where are meetings?

Meetings are held the third Tuesday of each month, 6:00 – 7:00 PM, in the Jefferson Elementary Library, 100 Princetown Rd., Schenectady. Use the Intermediate office entrance (the door in the bus lot).

Can't make it in person? Meeting links are posted to our Facebook group before each meeting. Meeting details →

Who can come to meetings?

All Jefferson families and school staff are welcome.

How do I donate?

Donate online to the Woestina Pre-K PTO or the Jefferson PTO, or see the Donate page.

What events and programs does the PTO run?

See everything coming up on the Jefferson PTO page or the calendar.

Schalmont Central PTO

Middle School & High School

When and where are meetings?

There are three meetings this school year, all on Thursdays from 6:00 – 7:00 PM at Schalmont Middle School, 2 Sabre Drive, Schenectady:

  • Fall: Thursday, October 22, in the Middle School Band Room
  • Winter: Thursday, January 14
  • Spring: Thursday, April 15

Join virtually on Google Meet, or dial (US) +1 724-259-7036, PIN 973 412 993#. Meeting details →

Who can come to meetings?

Meetings are open to all parents, guardians and school staff.

How do I donate?

Donate online to the Schalmont Central PTO, or see the Donate page.

How does the Holiday Catalog fundraiser work?

The sale runs Oct. 23 – Nov. 13, 2026, and orders are picked up Monday, Dec. 14, 4:30 – 7:30 PM in the High School Cafeteria. Browse the catalogs and see how to order on the Holiday Catalog page.
