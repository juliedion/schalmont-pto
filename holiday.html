How you can help

  • Show the catalogs to family and friends, and ask parents to take them to work.
  • Register online at profit-raisers.com or in the free MyFundR app, so you can send your fundraising link by email, text and social media.
  • Online orders count toward student prizes. They ship to our group and are delivered with your paper order, unless the customer picks a ship-to-home item.

Paper order forms — remember to:

  1. Make checks or money orders payable to Schalmont MS PTO.
  2. Do not collect tax.
  3. Fill in the top of the order form with your address, email and phone number.
  4. Print brochure item codes clearly.
  5. Return both parts of the order form by November 13, 2026.
