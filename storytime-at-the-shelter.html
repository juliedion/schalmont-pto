Read to the shelter animals

Help socialize and build trust for animals waiting on their furever families. Kids read or draw alongside cats and other small animals at the shelter.

Storytime at the Shelter benefits both children and animals. By reading aloud to shelter animals, children improve their literacy skills, gain confidence, and build empathy in a non-judgmental environment. At the same time, the comforting sound of a child's voice calms stressed animals, helps them develop trust and boosts chances of finding a forever home.

Please note: an adult is required to stay.
